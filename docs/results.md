## Key assumptions

* Seven daily periods (Oct 25–31); actuals for Oct 25–26; decision at the start of Oct 27 with the repair
  estimate (A1 down Oct 27 06:00 → Oct 29 14:00) known. No further unplanned downtime is assumed after that.
* Shifts 06:00–22:00 (16 standard h); overtime up to 4 h per line-day, only if the line is not down all day.
* Ready-to-ship cutoff: allocated by end of the deadline day. No allocation after a deadline (no late shipments).
* Transfer lead time 1 day; transferred bags count only at Northfield (the dispatch factory).
* The recovery planner is greedy earliest-deadline-first and capacity-only; materials are enforced at execution,
  with stock reserved for each factory's remaining baseline runs (net of scheduled receipts).
* Markdown on leftover seasonal stock is an explicit 50% assumption, shown separately, never added to cost.
